What we do

Built for local market administrators

The Public Market Stall Vendor System helps local government units manage their public market operations with less paperwork and more clarity.

Stall Management

Monitor all stalls in the market, track occupancy status, and manage stall assignments for registered vendors.

Vendor Profiles

Keep complete records of market vendors including contact details, stall assignments, and lease information.

Lease Contracts

Track lease agreements, contract durations, and renewal deadlines for every vendor in the market.

Fee Collections

Record daily rental and market fee collections from vendors and generate reports for accounting.

Utility Billing

Calculate and collect water and electricity charges per vendor stall based on actual consumption.

Violations Log

Record market rule violations and track fines or penalties imposed on vendors for compliance monitoring.
