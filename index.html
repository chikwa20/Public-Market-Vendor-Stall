About the System

The Public Market Stall Vendor System is developed to help local government market administrators and supervisors manage their public market operations more efficiently.

Stall Management

Monitor all stalls in the market, track occupancy, and manage stall assignments for vendors.

Vendor Records

Keep complete records of registered market vendors including their personal information and stall assignments.

Lease Contracts

Track lease agreements between the market administration and vendors, including contract dates and renewal status.

Daily Collections

Record daily rental and fee collections from vendors, and generate collection reports for accounting purposes.

System Features

✓ Manage vendor registrations and profiles
✓ Track stall occupancy and availability
✓ Monitor lease contract status and renewals
✓ Record and track daily fee collections
✓ Manage market collector accounts
✓ Generate basic collection reports
✓ View stall map and section layout
✓ Record utility billings and penalties
